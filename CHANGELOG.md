# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release.

- A lightweight standalone take on the Luxthos Experience Bar WeakAura: level, XP, completed quest XP,
  rested XP, time this level, session time, XP per hour and time to level.
- Settings page (`/seb`, right-click the bar, or Options > AddOns): move, resize and scale the bar, toggle
  each line of text, hide the default experience bar, and switch each visual effect on or off.
- A liquid fill that ripples, splashes and absorbs completed quest XP as you turn quests in.
- 25 themes: the original look, Class Color, Faction, Item Quality, Prismatic, colour themes, Outland zones
  and effect themes with their own particles.
- Supports WoW Classic Era, TBC Anniversary and WoW Forever.
